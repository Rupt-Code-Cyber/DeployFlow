# DeployFlow Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a professional, safe, and collaborative workspace for engineers across all skill groups, backgrounds, identities, and cultures.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other team members.
- Offering constructive feedback based on system data rather than personal opinions.
- Respecting distinct engineering view points during architectural reviews.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Intolerant commentary or harassment of any kind.
- Public or private harassment.
- Unprofessional behavior within shared tracking issues or communication zones.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards, and will handle any non-compliant behavior objectively and thoroughly.
